Targets use the same point scale as every activity.

1 point = 40 MET-minutes for activities with known duration and intensity. The practical 15-point target equals 600 MET-minutes. Daily steps use a separate conservative bonus: 0 points through 4,000 steps, then 0.5 point per additional 1,000 steps, capped at 4 points per day. Strength work is tracked separately against a goal of 2 days per week. Avoid logging the same walking movement both as steps and as Fast walking / hiking.
