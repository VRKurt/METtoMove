How the plan works

Audit week: light daily movement is tracked through steps, plus one gym session of at least 20 minutes and power/strength training of at least 15 minutes on two separate days.

Regular week: steps remain visible but do not count toward the aerobic minimum. Purposeful brisk walking, jogging or rowing must total at least 600 MET-minutes, equal to 15 points. No strength target is set.

1 point = 40 MET-minutes. Steps use the existing conservative bonus: 0 points through 4,000; then 0.5 point per additional 1,000, capped at 4 points per day.

Implementation assumption: “light activity” in an audit week is shown as 5 days with at least 10,000 steps. Change AUDIT_STEP_DAYS or AUDIT_STEP_THRESHOLD in the script if you prefer another threshold.
