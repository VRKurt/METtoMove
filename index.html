Weekly activity level

Targets use the same point scale as every activity.

1 point = 40 MET-minutes. At a 4 MET reference intensity, 10 minutes = 1 point. General daily steps are deliberately more conservative: the first 3,000 steps are baseline, and 10,000 total daily steps earn about 5.2 points. Use Fast walking / hiking for a purposeful walk, but do not log the same movement twice.
